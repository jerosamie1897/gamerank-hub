COMMUNITY RULES

Constructive player discussion

Ratings and comments should help another player understand an experience, decision, limitation, or useful solution.

What to contribute

  • Describe your platform, experience level, mode, or use case when it changes the context.
  • Explain why you agree or disagree instead of attacking another player.
  • Mark significant story spoilers clearly.
  • Correct factual claims with reliable sources when possible.
  • Keep ratings focused on the game experience rather than unrelated publisher disputes.

What is not accepted

  • Harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, or encouragement of harm.
  • Personal information, impersonation, account credentials, or attempts to identify another user.
  • Spam, undisclosed promotion, referral codes, repeated links, scams, cheats, malware, or account trading.
  • Copied reviews, automated submissions, review manipulation, or coordinated rating abuse.
  • Instructions that bypass security, purchases, bans, age controls, or platform rules.

Moderation

Comments are reviewed before publication. Moderators may approve, reject, edit only for necessary privacy removal, or later remove content that violates these guidelines. Ratings may be excluded when there is evidence of automation or manipulation.

These guidelines will evolve as the community grows. Material changes are recorded in the public update log.
