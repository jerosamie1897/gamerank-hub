PRIVACY

GameRank Hub privacy notice

The community system is designed to collect only the information needed for ratings, moderated comments, abuse prevention, and reliable operation.

Effective August 12, 2026

Information processed

When you submit a rating, the service stores the reviewed page, score, timestamp, and a pseudonymous browser identifier. When you submit a comment, it stores the reviewed page, display name, comment text, moderation status, timestamp, and the same type of pseudonymous identifier.

The server temporarily converts the requesting network address into a keyed one-way hash for rate limiting. Raw network addresses are not stored in the community database. Standard Nginx access and error logs contain network and request information for security, troubleshooting, and aggregate traffic measurement. Those logs rotate daily and are retained for up to 14 days. Aggregate reports do not include visitor network addresses.

Delivery and security provider

Cloudflare proxies site traffic to provide DNS, HTTPS delivery, caching, and security controls. Cloudflare may process connection and request information under its own privacy terms. GameRank Hub does not use this information for advertising profiles.

Cookies and purpose

The community service sets a first-party visitor cookie to limit a browser to one current rating per game and a short-lived CSRF cookie to prevent unauthorized form submissions. These cookies are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Moderation and retention

Comments remain pending until approved or rejected. Pending comments are removed after 30 days and rejected comments after 7 days. Approved comments and ratings remain until removed for moderation, correction, legal, privacy, or operational reasons. Rate-limit records are removed after approximately one day.

Your choices

Do not include an email address, real name, account identifier, location, or other sensitive information in a display name or comment. A monitored privacy contact must be published before actively promoting community submissions so users can request review or deletion.
