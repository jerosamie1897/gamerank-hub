ABOUT GAMERANK HUB

Game information with visible limits

GameRank Hub helps players judge whether a game fits their time, device, budget, and preferred way to play.

What the site publishes

Game profiles answer a practical decision: who the game is for, what a normal session asks from the player, where the learning or social friction appears, and what spending or progression systems deserve attention. They are not scored reviews unless a page explicitly documents hands-on testing.

Guides must solve one defined problem with a repeatable process, clear limitations, and a way for the reader to judge whether the advice worked. Pages are not published merely to target another keyword variation.

Evidence and sourcing

Platform availability, account requirements, business models, and service details should be traced to an official publisher, developer, platform, or support page. Each game profile identifies its primary source and the date the information was checked.

Editorial interpretation is labeled as such. When GameRank Hub has not performed a hands-on test, the page says so and avoids review scores, performance claims, or statements that require direct observation.

How pages are selected and updated

Coverage is prioritized when a page can provide a distinct decision framework rather than repeat generic gaming advice. Material updates revise the visible checked date, structured data, search index, and sitemap together. A date is not changed for cosmetic edits.

Live-service details can change after publication. Official store and support pages remain the source of truth for current prices, regions, system requirements, modes, and account terms.

Ownership and commercial policy

GameRank Hub is an independent project designed and developed by JTB. Rankings and placement are not sold. Any future sponsorship, free product, or affiliate relationship must be disclosed beside the affected content and cannot determine editorial conclusions.

Corrections

Report a factual error through the public GameRank Hub issue tracker. Include the page URL, the disputed statement, and a reliable source. Confirmed material errors should be corrected and recorded in the update log.
