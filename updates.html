CHANGELOG

GameRank Hub update log

Material changes are recorded here so readers and search engines can distinguish meaningful updates from minor formatting edits.

AUGUST 12, 2026

Traffic and publishing-model overhaul

  • Replaced 30 formulaic review pages with sourced before-you-play profiles focused on time, platform, social load, learning barriers, progression, and spending pressure.
  • Removed unsupported Review structured data and replaced it with Article, WebPage, VideoGame, HowTo, and breadcrumb markup appropriate to the available evidence.
  • Rebuilt all 15 guides and four blog posts around topic-specific checklists, scenarios, mistakes, success criteria, sources, and limitations.
  • Reworked the game-profile hub, homepage positioning, internal links, navigation, search index, RSS feed, and sitemap around distinct player decisions.
  • Added automated checks for duplicate titles, duplicate descriptions, repeated long-form paragraphs, unsupported review schema, missing source disclosures, and sitemap inconsistencies.

AUGUST 12, 2026

Community and international expansion

  • Added moderated player comments and one-per-browser ratings to all 30 game profile pages.
  • Added dynamic related-profile recommendations and a community activity page.
  • Added Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Arabic language hubs with hreflang support.
  • Added server-side SQLite storage, API rate limiting, CSRF protection, spam controls, and moderation endpoints.
  • Added IndexNow support and expanded sitemap coverage.

AUGUST 11, 2026

Initial content and technical SEO launch

  • Published the original 30 game pages, 15 practical guides, four blog posts, and an FAQ.
  • Added the first editorial illustration library and structured data. The review markup and repeated templates were replaced during the August 12 publishing-model overhaul.
  • Added RSS, robots directives, XML sitemap, HTTPS, canonical URLs, caching, redirects, and security headers.
