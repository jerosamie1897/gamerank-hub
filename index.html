HOW WE CHOOSE

Profiles designed around evidence and fit

Every profile separates sourced facts from editorial interpretation. It identifies the official source, the date checked, what can change, and whether the page includes hands-on testing.

The useful question is not whether a popular game is universally “good.” It is whether its sessions, systems, social demands, and costs match what a particular player wants.
